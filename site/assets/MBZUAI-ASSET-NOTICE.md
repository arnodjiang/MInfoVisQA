# MBZUAI institutional identity

`mbzuai-logo.svg` is an unmodified official English RGB navy logo from MBZUAI's
[primary logos archive](https://staticcdn.mbzuai.ac.ae/mbzuaiwpprd01/2026/02/MBZUAI-Primary-logos.zip),
linked by the [official media center](https://mbzuai.ac.ae/news-events/media-center).
Archive member: `RGB (for digital)/MBZUAI_Logo_EN_RGB/SVG/MBZUAI_Logo_EN_Navy_Blue_RGB.svg`.
Retrieved 2026-09-28. The mark remains the property of MBZUAI and is not covered
by this repository's code license.

The project maintainer identifies MBZUAI as an institution affiliated with the paper.
The logo identifies that affiliation; this page is a research project website.
Do not infer additional institutional endorsements from its inclusion.

Palette source: MBZUAI Brand Guidelines, March 2026, pages 27–28:
Navy Blue `#154677`, Sand `#E5C687`, White; light tints `#8AA2BB`, `#C5D1DD`,
`#F2E3C3`, `#F9F1E1`. The institutional logo is not recolored, cropped or redrawn.
It has clear space and keeps its original aspect ratio. The site uses the stated
font-family preference with locally available fallbacks; no licensed fonts are
redistributed. Real benchmark example images and reported scores are unchanged.
